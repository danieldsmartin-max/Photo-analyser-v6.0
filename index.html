Conservative submission policy

  • Reject/repair visible dust, sensor spots, banding, halos, compression, chromatic aberration and distracting noise at 100% view.
  • Do not assume high ISO means failure; inspect actual image noise and detail.
  • Do not infer color temperature, dynamic range in EV, authenticity, subject sharpness or legal rights from RGB pixels alone.
  • Commercial submissions generally need appropriate model/property releases for recognizable people or protected/private property; editorial rules differ.
  • Verify trademarks, logos, copyrighted artwork, privacy, location restrictions, AI-generation/editing disclosures and agency-specific policies.
  • Metadata must be accurate, relevant, non-spammy and free of unsupported claims; add title, description and keywords in the agency portal.
